# Razorglint Elite Bounty Pantry
**Qualification gates, structural regression oracles and portable evidence**

![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3.11%2B-3776AB?logo=python&logoColor=white) ![CI](https://img.shields.io/badge/CI-GitHub%20Actions-2088FF?logo=githubactions&logoColor=white) ![JSON](https://img.shields.io/badge/Data-JSON-334155?logo=json&logoColor=white) ![ZIP](https://img.shields.io/badge/Evidence-SHA--256%20ZIP-374151) ![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Internal%20qualification-334155)

Elite Pantry is an **independent private engineering workbench** for deciding whether an engineering opportunity and a reusable method are eligible for further work. It separates an interesting defect from an **authorized, funded, reproducible and transferable** assignment.

## Demonstrated modules

| Capability | Actual boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Qualification gates | Deterministic opportunity screening with explicit commercial and legal blockers |
| JSON oracle | Stable structural comparison, duplicate-key rejection, bounded differences and negative fixtures |
| Proof pack | Deterministic evidence ZIP + SHA-256 manifest; verification of packaged-byte consistency, **not source authenticity** |
| Method review | First-party method declarations tied to source anchors; missing or stale evidence remains blocked |
| BRE handoff | Strict **synthetic** review envelope accepted for inspection but deliberately re-blocked as `BLOCKED_MANUAL_REVIEW` |
| Learning intake | Synthetic metadata only; real customer/bounty cases are not automatically admitted or learned from |

**Qualification basis:** private synthetic and internal source-tests, including bounded component and handoff documentation through the 3 Nov 2026 repository state. The presence of a test, source match or candidate does **not** prove a paid issue was solved. There is **no confirmed bounty payout, customer fix or autonomous repair delivery**.

Elite Pantry and BRE remain separate products: **Pantry qualifies methods and commercial eligibility; BRE performs bounded engineering preflight and candidate verification**. Their handoff remains restricted and subject to human review.
